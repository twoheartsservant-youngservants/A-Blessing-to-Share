How a blessing travels

A few simple steps can turn your thoughts into an encouragement someone can keep.

  1. 1

    Design

    Choose a symbol, colors, pattern, border, and style. Then write a blessing in your own words.

  2. 2

    Share

    Save your creation and share it using its link, display a QR code, or send the blessing directly by email.

  3. 3

    Encourage

    Your churchmate opens the blessing and sees the card you created. No account is needed to view it.
